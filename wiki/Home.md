# Better NMSU Maps

CS 371 project by The Brainy Bunch.

Better NMSU Maps will be a campus map website for NMSU students. The main idea is you type in a room from your class schedule (like SH 118A) and it shows you the building, the room on the floor plan, and how to get there. No login and nothing to download.
