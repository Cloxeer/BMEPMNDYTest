# User Stories

Effort is in Fibonacci numbers, sized against Pictures of entrances (55). We are treating the whole project as about 377.

## 1: Pictures of entrances

Story: As a Freshman, I need pictures of the entrances of the building, so I can use them as landmarks for the map entrances.

Elaboration: Since the blueprints for the insides of the buildings have no indication of which entrance you are at we need to have a picture of the entrance, so you could cross reference with the entrance you are at.

Constraints: Photos have to be ones we take ourselves.

Effort Estimation: 55

Acceptance Test: Test every entrance picture that was stored and cross reference them with the real life entrance to make sure they match up and that it is easily recognizable.

## 2: Campus map

Story: As a new student, I want to see a campus map with the buildings marked so that I know where my classes are.

Elaboration: Tapping a building will show its name, address and building code.

Constraints: Has to work on a phone with no login.

Effort Estimation: 21

Acceptance Test: Open the map on a phone and check the buildings are marked and their info matches NMSU's records.

## 3: Search for a room

Story: As a student, I want to search a room the way it's written on my schedule (like SH 118A) so that I can find my class.

Elaboration: Searching a room will take you to its building.

Constraints: Rooms will come from NMSU's class schedule.

Effort Estimation: 34

Acceptance Test: Search SH 118A and check it goes to Science Hall.

## 4: Floor plans

Story: As a student in a new building, I want to see the floor plan so that I can find my room.

Elaboration: Each building will show its floor plan with the rooms labeled.

Constraints: Floor plans will come from the evacuation maps posted in each building.

Effort Estimation: 55

Acceptance Test: Open a building and check its floor plan matches the posted map.

## 5: Directions

Story: As a student running late, I want directions to my class so that I get there on time.

Elaboration: The map will show a walking route from where you are to the building.

Constraints: Needs location permission.

Effort Estimation: 55

Acceptance Test: Get directions to a building and walk the route to check it gets you there.

## 6: Parking

Story: As a commuter student, I want to see where I'm allowed to park so that I don't get a ticket.

Elaboration: Parking lots will show their permit color.

Constraints: Lots and permits will come from NMSU Parking Services.

Effort Estimation: 21

Acceptance Test: Check five lots on the map against NMSU's parking map.
